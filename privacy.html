Privacy policy

MiraGaming Uploader is used only by the owner of the MiRa Gaming Channel to upload that channel's own videos.

  • It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the owner's channel.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to anyone else.
  • It does not collect, store or share any data about other people.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
